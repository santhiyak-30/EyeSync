# EyeSync: Failure Mode & Effects Analysis (FMEA)

## Overview
Failure Mode and Effects Analysis (FMEA) is a systematic, proactive method for evaluating screening camp operational workflows to identify where and how they might fail, and to assess the relative impact of different failures.

---

## FMEA Risk Assessment Matrix

| # | Failure Mode | Root Cause in Field Camp Operations | Automated Detection Method | User & Patient Impact | Clinical Risk Level | EyeSync System Defense Response | Recommended Clinician Action |
| :- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Missing Molecular Evidence** | Central molecular genetics backlog; reagent supply shortage; tube lost before accessioning. | Completeness engine checks absence of molecular record for active case. | Clinician risks misinterpreting missing PCR as a confirmed negative finding. | **High (False Negative Risk)** | Displays warning: *"Molecular evidence unavailable. Do NOT assume negative result."* Score capped at 80%. | Order reflex multiplex PCR or document justification for visual-only management plan. |
| **2** | **Low-Quality Imaging** | Dense cataracts, poor pupil dilation, patient blink, or dirty camera objective lens. | Automated image quality analyzer scores capture below 60/100 threshold. | Reviewer unable to discern microaneurysms, neovascularization, or cup-to-disc ratio. | **High (Diagnostic Uncertainty)** | Displays warning: *"Low-quality imaging – interpretation confidence reduced."* Case flagged as `REVIEW_REQUIRED`. | Re-image patient in screening pod with pupil dilation or refer for slit-lamp biomicroscopy. |
| **3** | **Stale Molecular Result** | Patient delayed returning to camp; molecular assay completed >30 days prior. | Freshness engine evaluates age of `result_at` timestamp exceeding 30-day window. | Clinician bases treatment on obsolete pathogen viral load that may have cleared or surged. | **Medium (Temporal Discordance)** | Displays warning: *"STALE – verify before clinical review."* Amber badge rendered in timeline. | Correlate with acute ocular redness/pain; order rapid repeat tear-film biomarker test. |
| **4** | **Duplicate Imaging Evidence** | Technician re-took scan due to blink without invalidating the initial erroneous capture. | Duplicate modality check identifies multiple captures for same eye in single session. | Reviewer confused about which image represents the definitive diagnostic capture. | **Low (Workflow Inefficiency)** | Displays alert: *"DUPLICATE EVIDENCE DETECTED."* Shows both captures with comparative quality scores. | Review timestamps and quality scores; designate higher quality scan as the primary record. |
| **5** | **Conflicting Evidence** | Biopsy taken from lesion margin; biological discordance; or mislabeled specimen. | Clinical rule engine flags Pathology Severe/Abnormal finding paired with Imaging Normal read. | High risk of premature patient discharge or contradictory treatment recommendations. | **Critical (Diagnostic Discordance)** | Displays crimson alert: *"CONFLICTING EVIDENCE – REVIEW REQUIRED."* Blocks automated clearance. | Mandatory multidisciplinary case conference. Joint review of histology and fundus angiogram. |
| **6** | **Broken Specimen Lineage** | Smudged handwritten tube label during transport; barcode scanner sync loss. | Lineage engine detects missing or lost accession linkage between field swab and lab intake. | Results could belong to a different patient; catastrophic misattribution risk. | **Critical (Chain-of-Custody Failure)** | Displays warning: *"SPECIMEN LINEAGE INCOMPLETE / LINEAGE BROKEN."* Red broken step in lineage tree. | Quarantine unauthenticated specimen immediately. Audit field collection log and redraw sample. |
