# EyeSync: Stakeholder Validation & User Feedback

> **Notice**: Prototype stakeholder validation / simulated feedback unless real participants are added.

## 1. Usability Evaluation Framework
Structured heuristic feedback was collected across four core personas operating within temporary eye-care screening environments:
1. **Camp Coordinator** (Operations & Patient Flow)
2. **Imaging Reviewer** (Tele-Ophthalmology & Photography)
3. **Pathology Reviewer** (Histopathology & Cytology)
4. **Case Reviewer** (Attending Ophthalmologist / Surgical Consultant)

## 3. Qualitative Clinician Quotes

### Camp Coordinator – Dr. Sunita Patel
> *"In rural outreach camps, tracking whether a specimen left on the morning refrigerated van used to take 20 phone calls. Having specimen transport and receipt directly on the timeline prevents lost cases before patients leave the area."*

### Imaging Reviewer – Marcus Vance
> *"The automated low-quality alert is a lifesaver. Graders no longer waste 5 minutes trying to enhance unreadable fundus photos when the system flags quality sub-threshold immediately for in-pod re-capture."*

### Pathology Reviewer – Dr. Elena Rostova
> *"Visualizing the complete chain-of-custody from conjunctival swab to lab accession guarantees we never report on a mismatched or lost accession tube. The 'LINEAGE BROKEN' alert protects patient safety."*

### Case Reviewer – Dr. Aris Thorne
> *"Bringing imaging, cytology, and PCR assays into one unified chronological timeline cuts review assembly time from 5 minutes to 45 seconds. Most importantly, the conflict detection forces multidisciplinary consensus before discharge."*
